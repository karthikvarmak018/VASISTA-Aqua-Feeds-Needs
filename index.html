THE SCIENCE BEHIND MOLASSES

Why Molasses is Essential for
Shrimp Feeds & Pond Water

Molasses provides a vital organic carbon source that transforms pond water dynamics and accelerates shrimp weight gain.

High Brix 80°+
Unadulterated & Filtered

1. Improves Pond Water Quality

• Ammonia & Nitrite Control: Provides digestible carbon to stimulate heterotrophic bacteria, rapidly converting toxic ammonia (TAN) into harmless microbial protein.

• Biofloc & Plankton Stability: Optimizes the Carbon-to-Nitrogen (C:N ratio at 10:1–15:1), preventing harmful blue-green algae crashes and water fouling.

2. Enhances Shrimp Growth Performance

• Feed Attractant & Natural Binder: Sweet aroma and natural stickiness enhance feed palatability, reducing pellet wastage and feed loss on pond bottoms.

• Gut Health & Faster Molting: Rich in essential carbohydrates, potassium, and trace minerals that nourish beneficial gut microflora and accelerate daily weight gain.
