## 🎯 Core Value & Purpose

### 1. Improves Water Quality
- **Ammonia & Nitrite Reduction:** Stimulates heterotrophic bacteria to break down toxic Total Ammonia Nitrogen (TAN) and Nitrite ($NO_2^-$).
- **Biofloc & Carbon-to-Nitrogen Balance:** Maintains optimal C:N ratio (10:1–15:1), stabilizing beneficial plankton and preventing sudden algal crashes.

### 2. Enhances Shrimp Growth Performance
- **Feed Attractant & Binder:** Natural aroma and stickiness maximize feed intake and minimize pellet loss on the pond bottom.
- **Gut Vitality & Rapid Molting:** Provides essential carbohydrates, potassium, and trace minerals for healthy gut microflora and faster body weight gain.
